Climate · method 2.0 · as of 2025 · ex ante

Two ledgers, never netted: what the land stores, and what the programmes change.

The inventory ledger counts carbon stock changes inside programme boundaries. The intervention ledger counts the net benefit against a baseline, after leakage and a non-permanence buffer. Bands are the 5th to 95th percentile of 2,000 draws over the published uncertainties.
